# Merchant eligibility policy

Localmart is a marketplace for independently owned local retail businesses. To list on Localmart, a business must meet all of the following criteria:

## Required

**Local ownership** — 51% or more of the business must be owned by one or more individuals who reside in or whose principal place of business is within the community's defined geographic area.

**Operational control** — The local owner must hold full decision-making authority over the business name, branding, purchasing decisions, staffing, and day-to-day operations. No external entity may restrict or control these decisions.

**Scale** — The business may have no more than six retail locations, all within a single US state.

**Independence** — The business must not be a subsidiary, division, or franchisee of a regional or national brand. The business must not be publicly traded or majority-owned by a private equity firm or outside investor without community presence.

**Physical presence** — The business must have a brick-and-mortar retail location within the community's defined geographic boundary.

## Co-operative memberships

Businesses that are members of retailer-owned co-operatives (such as Ace Hardware or True Value) qualify provided the local owner retains full operational independence. Consumer co-operatives with national scale (such as REI) do not qualify.

## Franchise arrangements

Traditional franchise arrangements where a franchisor controls core business operations, branding, or purchasing do not qualify, regardless of local ownership.

## Verification

Eligibility is self-declared by the merchant at signup. Community moderators review all applications and may request supporting documentation. Misrepresentation of eligibility results in immediate removal from the platform.
